Interpreting odds_ratios.csv

baseline odds = 0.077 ~ 1/13. This means that about 1 couple conveive for every 13 couples who don't, for the reference group (18-25 yeears old, low income, low education etc...)

The Odds Ratios then compare how much the odds change for the other groups relative to the reference group.

For example, the OR for the both_jobless row is 0.778. this means that for every 1 reference group couple that conceives, only 0.778 couple from the both_jobless group conceive, where all other traits are held constant (e.g. same age group, income, education etc). And the overall odds for the both_jobless group can be found as 0.778 x (0.077). 

The OR for the both_jobless row is 0.778. This means that where 1 reference-group couple conceives for every 13 that don't, only 0.778 jobless couples conceive for every 13 that don't, with all other traits held constant (same age group, income, education, origin). The odds for a jobless couple that is otherwise in the reference categories are 0.778 × (1/13) = 0.060; for any other jobless couple, multiply further by the odds ratios of its other traits.
